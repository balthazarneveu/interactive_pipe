Build an interactive mini camera pipeline with interactive_pipe: start from a synthetic Bayer raw image (a colourful scene, mosaiced, with a colour cast and some noise) and go through black level, demosaicing, white balance and gamma. I want students to understand why the order of the stages matters and what each stage does, so each stage needs its own controls, grouped by stage, and I want to see intermediate results, not only the final image. Moving a late-stage slider should not recompute the early stages.

Line budget: 90.
