Build an interactive demo with interactive_pipe, using PyTorch, where a small MLP is trained on a 2D toy classification dataset (two moons or spirals, with a noise slider) and the decision boundary is displayed with the data points on top, next to the training and validation loss curves. Controls for width, depth, weight decay and number of training steps. Students should see under-fitting, over-fitting and the effect of regularization. Training has to be fast enough to stay interactive, and changing a display-only setting must not retrain the network.

Line budget: 90. PyTorch allowed.
