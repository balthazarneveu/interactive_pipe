Build an interactive demo with interactive_pipe of what happens to an image when a pinhole camera rotates in place: the image is warped by the homography K R K^-1. Use a synthetic scene where distortion is easy to read (a labelled grid or checkerboard), with yaw, pitch and roll sliders and a focal length slider. Students should see that the same rotation moves the image more at long focal length, and how the edges stretch at wide field of view. Show the source next to the warped view.

Line budget: 70.
